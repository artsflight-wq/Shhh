COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Last Updated: November 25, 2025

STATUS: Public Community Spaces
ACCESS: Applies to all members
ENFORCEMENT: By community moderators and administrators

Based on and adapted from Discord's Community Guidelines

================================================================================

INTRODUCTION

These Community Guidelines define behavioral standards for our community. 
They ensure that everyone can express themselves and find community without 
compromising anyone else's safety or well-being.

Everyone participating in our community must follow these guidelines. They apply 
to all interactions, content, and behaviors in public and private spaces.

If you encounter violations, please report them to community moderators.

================================================================================

SECTION 1: RESPECT EACH OTHER

================================================================================

1. HARASSMENT AND BULLYING

DO NOT:
- Engage in targeted harassment of individuals or groups
- Participate in bullying campaigns
- Coordinate harassment across channels or platforms
- Use "raids" to harass community members
- Make repeated unwanted contact after being asked to stop
- Spread rumors or false information to harm someone's reputation
- Publicly mock or ridicule members
- Targeted personal attacks

This includes:
- Continued targeting after requests to stop
- Bank or block evasion to continue harassment
- Coordinated group harassment
- Harassment of moderators or admins
- Targeted campaigns against specific members

================================================================================

2. THREATS AND VIOLENCE

DO NOT:
- Make threats against individuals or groups
- Make threats of violence, harm, or illegal action
- Make veiled or conditional threats
- Encourage others to harm someone
- Share plans to commit violence
- Glorify or celebrate violence
- Make threats during disagreements or disputes
- Threaten to harm property or livelihood
- Issue death threats, rape threats, or assault threats

================================================================================

3. PERSONALLY IDENTIFIABLE INFORMATION (DOXXING)

DO NOT:
- Share someone's personal information without consent
- Share addresses, phone numbers, or real names (without permission)
- Share workplace or school information
- Share financial information
- Share social media accounts
- Create posts designed to spread someone's information
- Facilitate doxxing by others
- Sell or purchase personal information
- Encourage others to find or share someone's information

This is strictly prohibited regardless of public availability.

================================================================================

4. HATE SPEECH AND HATEFUL CONDUCT

DO NOT:
- Use hate speech targeting protected characteristics
- Attack people based on race, ethnicity, national origin, skin color, or religion
- Attack people based on sexual orientation or gender identity
- Attack people based on disability, disease, or health status
- Attack people based on political beliefs
- Use slurs or dehumanizing language
- Use hate symbols or imagery
- Deny historical atrocities or mass human suffering
- Make generalized attacks on groups of people
- Promote hatred or violence against groups

Protected characteristics include (but are not limited to):
- Race and ethnicity
- Color and skin tone
- National origin and citizenship
- Religion and belief systems
- Sexual orientation and gender identity
- Sex and gender
- Disability (physical and mental)
- Disease and health status
- Age
- Caste and familial status
- Political beliefs and affiliations

================================================================================

5. VIOLENT EXTREMISM

DO NOT:
- Promote, coordinate, or participate in violent extremism
- Share manifestos or propaganda from violent groups
- Glorify or celebrate violence or violent actors
- Encourage extremist ideology or radicalization
- Coordinate violent acts
- Recruit members for extremist causes
- Share extremist content
- Promote conspiracy theories that incite violence
- Endorse violence against government or political groups
- Participate in extremist organizations

This applies to extremism across all ideologies and geographies.

================================================================================

6. CHILD SEXUAL ABUSE MATERIAL (CSAM)

ABSOLUTELY PROHIBITED:
- Any images or videos depicting child sexual abuse
- Any text, drawings, or animations depicting child sexual abuse
- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM), whether real, fictional, or AI-generated
- Grooming behavior toward minors
- Sexual contact with minors
- Solicitation of CSAM
- Distribution of CSAM

We report all suspected CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children 
and law enforcement.

================================================================================

7. TEEN AND CHILD SAFETY

IF YOU ARE UNDER 18:

DO NOT:
- Engage in sexual conduct or sexual interactions
- Share sexual or suggestive images or content
- Participate in dating spaces (community-moderated)
- Engage in behavior that puts your safety at risk
- Accept private messages from unknown adults
- Share personal information with strangers

DO:
- Report any adults attempting to contact you sexually
- Tell a trusted adult if someone makes you uncomfortable
- Use safety features available in your settings
- Take breaks if community participation becomes stressful

IF YOU ARE AN ADULT:

DO NOT:
- Engage sexually with anyone under 18
- Solicit sexual content from minors
- Share sexual content with minors
- Groom, exploit, or abuse minors
- Create dating spaces that facilitate teen interactions
- Produce CSAM in any form

================================================================================

8. SEXUAL CONDUCT AND CONTENT

DO NOT:
- Share sexually explicit content in non-adult channels
- Share sexual content in spaces accessible to minors
- Make unwanted sexual advances or solicitations
- Engage in sexual roleplay in public channels
- Share intimate media without consent (revenge porn)
- Create sexually explicit content involving real people without consent
- Share sexual content of anyone without their explicit consent

DO:
- Use age-restricted channels for adult content when available
- Age-verify before accessing adult content
- Ensure all adult content is consensually shared
- Respect others' boundaries regarding sexual discussion

================================================================================

9. SELF-HARM AND SUICIDE

DO NOT:
- Share content that encourages self-harm
- Provide instructions for self-harm methods
- Glorify or romanticize suicide or self-harm
- Share content that normalizes eating disorders
- Make light of self-harm or mental health crises
- Encourage others to self-harm
- Use self-harm as emotional manipulation or coercion
- Share graphic self-harm content

DO:
- Take suicide threats seriously
- Report concerning mental health discussions to moderators
- Share mental health resources when appropriate
- Support members in crisis
- Direct people to professional help

Resources:
- National Suicide Prevention Lifeline: 988
- Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741

================================================================================

10. GRAPHIC VIOLENCE AND GORE

DO NOT:
- Share images or videos of graphic violence
- Post gore or extremely violent content
- Share animal cruelty content
- Upload disturbing imagery intended to shock or harass
- Share real violence or accidents
- Use violent imagery as shock value

DO:
- Warn members before sharing potentially disturbing content (if necessary for legitimate purpose)
- Keep violent content related to legitimate discussion
- Respect trigger warnings and sensitivity needs

================================================================================

SECTION 2: RESPECT THE COMMUNITY

================================================================================

11. SPAM AND UNWANTED MESSAGES

DO NOT:
- Send unsolicited bulk messages to multiple users
- Spam channels with repetitive content
- Promote products or services repeatedly
- Send phishing or scam messages
- Sell services like spambots or raid tools
- Create automated systems to spam the community
- Hijack conversations for advertising
- Post the same message multiple times

DO:
- Use appropriate channels for announcements
- Ask before promoting projects
- Keep promotional content minimal
- Respect community guidelines about self-promotion

================================================================================

12. BOTS AND AUTOMATION

DO NOT:
- Use self-bots (bots using user accounts)
- Create automated accounts pretending to be human
- Use automation to artificially inflate engagement
- Automate harassment or spam
- Use bots to bypass community rules

================================================================================

13. PLATFORM MANIPULATION

DO NOT:
- Artificially inflate server membership
- Manipulate engagement metrics artificially
- Buy or sell Discord accounts, servers, or roles
- Trade Discord assets or permissions
- Participate in coordinated inauthentic behavior
- Use fake engagement services
- Create fake activity or followers

================================================================================

14. IMPERSONATION AND IDENTITY FRAUD

DO NOT:
- Pretend to be someone you're not
- Impersonate community staff or moderators
- Create fake profiles of real people
- Use misleading usernames intended to deceive
- Claim to represent organizations you don't represent
- Use deceptive practices to gain trust

================================================================================

15. MISINFORMATION AND DISINFORMATION

DO NOT:
- Spread false information knowingly
- Share misleading content designed to deceive
- Spread conspiracy theories that could cause harm
- Distribute misinformation about public health
- Share false information about elections or voting
- Create panic with false claims
- Deliberately distort facts

DO:
- Check sources before sharing
- Fact-check surprising claims
- Correct misinformation when you discover it
- Share from reputable sources

================================================================================

16. DECEPTIVE PRACTICES AND SCAMS

DO NOT:
- Attempt to scam or defraud community members
- Engage in financial scams or schemes
- Attempt phishing attacks
- Create fake opportunities to steal information
- Participate in price gouging
- Create counterfeit products
- Facilitate money laundering
- Provide tools that enable fraud

================================================================================

17. SECURITY BREACHES

DO NOT:
- Attempt to access accounts without authorization
- Phish for credentials or personal information
- Distribute malware or viruses
- Use denial-of-service attacks
- Attempt to compromise server security
- Share security vulnerabilities publicly
- Exploit bugs for unauthorized access
- Try to crack passwords or bypass authentication

================================================================================

18. EVADING ENFORCEMENT

DO NOT:
- Create new accounts to evade a ban
- Use alternate accounts after removal
- Attempt to circumvent suspension
- Ask others to help you evade enforcement
- Impersonate a banned user
- Return to community after permanent removal

================================================================================

19. FALSE REPORTS

DO NOT:
- Submit false reports to community staff
- Make up violations that didn't occur
- Report content without legitimate concern
- Abuse the reporting system
- Coordinate false reports
- Make false support requests

Abuse of the reporting system may result in your removal.

================================================================================

20. ABUSE OF SUPPORT SYSTEMS

DO NOT:
- Submit multiple false reports
- Spam support channels
- Make abusive requests to staff
- Threaten or harass moderators
- Demand unreasonable actions
- Create fake tickets

================================================================================

SECTION 3: FOLLOW APPLICABLE LAWS

================================================================================

ALL MEMBERS MUST COMPLY WITH APPLICABLE LAWS

Members must follow all local, state, national, and international laws, 
including (but not limited to):

- Laws prohibiting harassment and threats
- Laws protecting children and minors
- Laws prohibiting fraud and scams
- Intellectual property and copyright laws
- Laws prohibiting illegal goods or services
- Laws against discrimination
- Laws prohibiting violence
- Laws regulating financial transactions
- Laws protecting personal privacy
- Any laws applicable in your jurisdiction and the jurisdiction of community members

================================================================================

ENFORCEMENT

================================================================================

ENFORCEMENT PHILOSOPHY

We take Community Guidelines seriously. Violations result in enforcement action 
based on severity.

LEVELS OF ENFORCEMENT:

1. WARNINGS
- First-time minor violations
- Educational notice to correct behavior
- Reminder of community expectations

2. TEMPORARY SUSPENSION
- Repeated minor violations
- First-time moderate violations
- Duration typically 24 hours to 7 days
- Account access restricted during suspension

3. PERMANENT REMOVAL
- Serious violations (threats, CSAM, harassment campaigns)
- Repeated violations after suspension
- Illegal activity
- Violations causing community harm

4. APPEALS PROCESS
Users may appeal removals by:
- Contacting moderators
- Providing explanation
- Acknowledging the violation
- Demonstrating understanding of rules
- Committing to future compliance

Appeals are not guaranteed to succeed and are at moderator discretion.

ZERO TOLERANCE VIOLATIONS:

Immediate permanent removal for:
- Child sexual abuse material
- Serious threats or violence
- Illegal activity
- Coordinated harassment campaigns

================================================================================

REPORTING

If you witness violations:
1. Report to community moderators
2. Provide clear description and evidence
3. Do not engage with violators
4. Do not retaliate or counterattack
5. Be truthful in your report

To report:
- Use report features in Discord
- Contact moderators via DM
- Use designated reporting channels

================================================================================

ACKNOWLEDGMENT

By participating in our community, you:
- Agree to follow these guidelines
- Accept enforcement actions for violations
- Commit to respectful participation
- Understand removal is possible for violations

Remember: These guidelines exist to make our community welcoming and safe 
for everyone. Thank you for helping us maintain a positive environment.
