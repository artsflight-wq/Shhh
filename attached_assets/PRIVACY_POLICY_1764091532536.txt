PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: November 25, 2025

INTRODUCTION

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how the Discord Bot ("Bot", "Service", "we", "our", "us") collects, uses, processes, and protects your information when you use the Bot. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

By using the Bot, you consent to the collection and use of information as outlined in this Privacy Policy.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

1.1 AUTOMATIC COLLECTION
When you use the Bot, we automatically collect:
- Discord user ID and username
- Discord server (guild) ID
- Discord channel ID where commands are executed
- Timestamp of command execution
- Command names and parameters
- Message content (when applicable to Bot functionality)
- User profile information (public Discord profile data)

1.2 VOLUNTARY INFORMATION
You may voluntarily provide:
- Server configuration settings
- Custom preferences and options
- User-generated content (through commands like media captioning, custom settings)
- Support or feedback information

1.3 TECHNICAL INFORMATION
- IP addresses (via Discord API)
- Device information (indirectly through Discord)
- Browser or client information
- Bot usage statistics and analytics

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use collected information to:
- Provide, maintain, and improve the Bot functionality
- Personalize your experience and user preferences
- Process commands and fulfill requests
- Store user data for engagement tracking (XP, levels, streaks, pledges)
- Enforce our Terms of Service and prevent abuse
- Protect against fraud, abuse, and security threats
- Comply with legal obligations
- Analyze usage patterns and improve features
- Send notifications related to the Bot (when applicable)

3. DATA STORAGE AND RETENTION

3.1 STORAGE LOCATION
Your data is stored in secure databases maintained by third-party services including MongoDB Atlas and Redis hosting providers.

3.2 RETENTION PERIOD
- Command history and logs: Retained for up to 90 days
- User profile data (XP, levels, streaks): Retained while the Bot is active in your server
- Server configuration settings: Retained until manually deleted or Bot removal
- Engagement data: Retained indefinitely unless deleted by server administrators

3.3 DATA DELETION
You may request deletion of your data by:
- Removing the Bot from your server
- Requesting data deletion through support channels
- Data associated with deleted accounts may be retained as required by law

4. DATA SECURITY

We implement security measures including:
- Encryption of sensitive data in transit (TLS/SSL)
- Secure database connections with authentication
- Limited access to data by authorized personnel only
- Regular security updates and patches
- Monitoring for unauthorized access attempts

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

5. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The Bot integrates with third-party services:

5.1 DISCORD
The Bot operates through Discord and relies on Discord's infrastructure. Your use is subject to Discord's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

5.2 EXTERNAL APIS
The Bot may access external services including:
- YouTube Data API (for video searches)
- Spotify Web API (for music data)
- GitHub API (for user profiles)
- Roblox API (for user data)
- Google Search API (for web searches)

These services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their data practices.

5.3 DATABASE PROVIDERS
- MongoDB Atlas: Data storage and management
- Redis: Caching and session management

6. DATA SHARING

6.1 WE DO NOT SELL YOUR DATA
We do not sell, rent, lease, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties for their independent use.

6.2 INFORMATION WE MAY SHARE
We may share information with:
- Service providers who assist in Bot operations (under data processing agreements)
- Discord and Discord-related services as required
- Third-party API providers (only data necessary for the specific service)
- Legal authorities if required by law, legal process, or government request
- Other users in your Discord server (public server data)

6.3 AGGREGATE DATA
We may share anonymous, aggregated statistics that cannot identify you personally.

7. COOKIES AND TRACKING

The Bot does not use cookies. However, Discord's services may use cookies and tracking technologies. Please refer to Discord's Privacy Policy for more information.

8. USER RIGHTS

8.1 ACCESS
You have the right to request access to your personal data collected by the Bot.

8.2 CORRECTION
You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.

8.3 DELETION
You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.

8.4 PORTABILITY
You may request your data in a portable format.

To exercise these rights, contact us through the support channels.

9. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The Bot is not directed toward children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information and terminate the child's account.

Discord's Terms of Service require users to be at least 13 years old (or older in certain jurisdictions).

10. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS
